## 2024-07-29 - Prevent Stream Deadlocks and DoS in Process Execution
**Vulnerability:** In Crystal, sequentially reading `process.output` and `process.error` followed by `process.wait` can lead to stream deadlocks where a child process fills up the OS buffer for one stream while the parent waits on the other. Unbounded script execution without timeouts can also lead to Denial of Service (DoS).
**Learning:** In order to prevent this, the standard pattern in Crystal involves reading `stdout` and `stderr` concurrently using `spawn` and `Channel(String)`, and waiting for the process to complete using `select` on a `Channel(Process::Status)` with a timeout mechanism. Additionally, `io.skip_to_end` must be used after truncating the output to prevent child processes from hanging when writing to a full pipe.
**Prevention:** Always use concurrent stream reading (`spawn`/`Channel`), `select` with timeouts for `process.wait`, and `io.skip_to_end` when truncating IO streams from child processes to avoid deadlocks and resource exhaustion.
